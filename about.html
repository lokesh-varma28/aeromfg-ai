Engineering Pedigree & Mission

Built for High-Consequence Aerospace Manufacturing

AeroMFG AI was established by aerospace systems engineers and industrial IoT architects to solve the critical gap between physical shop-floor assembly and digital flight assurance.

Standard enterprise IoT platforms fail under the extreme conditions of aerospace manufacturing: complex multi-acre metallic reflection environments, ISO-classified cleanroom outgassing restrictions, volatile propulsion areas, and unyielding zero-defect flight certification mandates. AeroMFG AI was built specifically to excel in these environments.

Our Institutional Mission

To empower aerospace manufacturers and space vehicle integrators with real-time operational intelligence, ensuring that every flight-critical component, calibrated tool, and cleanroom procedure meets exacting zero-tolerance quality standards.

  • Bridging the gap between physical assembly lines and digital ERP/MES systems.
  • Preventing schedule slippage and multi-million dollar hardware damage.

Our Engineering Vision

To cultivate an aerospace production ecosystem where every subassembly, environmental condition, and assembly action is autonomously tracked, verified, and preserved in an immutable digital thread from sub-tier supplier to orbital launch.

  • Real-time spatial awareness without electromagnetic interference (EMI).
  • Autonomous visual quality inspection at production cadence.

Aerospace Engineering Principles

Our architecture adheres strictly to the highest defense and commercial flight benchmarks.

Cleanroom Safe (Outgassing Verified)

All sensors, tags, and gateway enclosures are fabricated from low-outgassing polymers tested to ASTM E595 standards, safe for ISO Class 5 optical payload integration.

EMI & RF Compliance

Transmitters operate within stringent aerospace power envelopes, preventing electromagnetic interference with sensitive satellite payloads and aircraft avionics.

Air-Gapped & On-Premise Ready

Complete edge intelligence stack deploys within private government-cloud or local air-gapped server clusters, satisfying strict ITAR and defense data sovereignty mandates.
