Enterprise Security, Cleanroom Compliance & Governance

A compliance-first architecture engineered to protect Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), sensitive space vehicle telemetry, and proprietary tooling blueprints.

In aerospace defense and commercial space programs, data security is indistinguishable from operational safety. AeroMFG AI implements hardware-rooted identity, granular role-based authorization, and air-gapped cryptographic tunnels built to withstand rigorous federal and international scrutiny.

Zero-Trust Edge IAM Architecture

Every sensor beacon, optical camera, gateway hub, and technician terminal possesses an immutable cryptographic identity rooted in hardware TPM 2.0 enclaves.

  • Mutual TLS (mTLS 1.3) enforced on all edge-to-server communications.
  • Automatic node revocation upon detection of RF tampering or physical disconnection.
  • Granular role-based access control (RBAC) segmented by security clearance levels.

ITAR & NIST 800-171 Compliance

Designed specifically for programs bound by International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR) and Department of Defense cybersecurity mandates.

  • Self-contained on-premise deployment mode with zero outbound internet egress.
  • AES-256 GCM encryption at rest with FIPS 140-3 validated key storage modules.
  • Audit trail logging meeting DFARS 252.204-7012 and CMMC Level 2 controls.
